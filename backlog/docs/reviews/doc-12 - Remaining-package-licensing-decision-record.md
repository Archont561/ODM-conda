# Remaining package licensing decision record

Scope: **the pinned Linux CPU builds listed below**, source and binary distribution via the existing channel, as of 2026-10-29. This is an engineering review packet, not legal advice or permission to redistribute. Do not set any release flag or publish on this packet alone; owner and qualified review remain necessary. Review precise output artifacts and dependency solve again if the commit, build, dependency versions, distribution arrangement, or network deployment changes. Four already published packages (dem2points, fpcfilter, odm-orthophoto, fastrasterfilter) are outside this new-package packet; their prior recorded owner decisions are in doc-6, not newly reapproved here.

## Cross-package actions and approval boundary

1. **Actual distribution inventory:** capture exact artifact SHA256 and its build/host/run dependency solve, link inputs (including headers/static linkage), packaged notice bytes and any separately distributed environment. License metadata alone is not an SBOM. Historical runs expire and are not durable offers.
2. **Source delivery:** for each proposed release, review actual source and scripts needed for the distributed binary, preserve notices and modification records, and provide authorized corresponding source at a durable release-specific location *before* binary publication. An archive hash or a URL template is not a legal completeness decision. Do not distribute restricted OpenMVS/MVE sources without a confirmed permitted route.
3. **Review and owner decisions:** resolve the individual rights and policy questions above with owner and qualified review where necessary; record exact version/build and evidence in the corresponding task. A passing test cannot decide a grant or replace approval. Only then may an owner-authorized change enable the existing release checks; publication is separately authorized.
4. **Technical release readiness:** rebuild/retest/audit the exact candidate commit after notice changes; verify relocation and relevant functionality separately. No local-only prototype is production validation. Track the selected service deployment and AGPL network obligations independently.

## Evidence and limitations

For this review the exact codeload source archives for dem2mesh, RenderDEM, PoissonRecon, and all five texturing sources were re-fetched and matched against `recipes/components.json`. For dem2mesh, 53 regular files were inventoried and the static JMeshLib CMake inputs reviewed; `src/Logger.h` lacks a separate leading grant. `Simplify.h` records an MIT attribution to Sven Forstmann; its archived project [source at `f4ad4b7`](https://github.com/sp4cerat/Fast-Quadric-Mesh-Simplification/blob/f4ad4b7c1c2b0cb80ae8927254b5b26e0f2eaf29/src.cmd/Simplify.h) carries the same attribution header. The project added its full MIT license in 2019, after the 2018 dem2mesh first commit; SHA256 `0740ee1d8869b715e5d492ec4961a415a543f4a556d79b3a9ba6fda4d38c6fd0`. That is corroborating notice evidence, not proof that the later copyright text alone covers every contributor or exact vendored revision. The candidate source patch and recipe include both the source header and that full text, and audit checks its exact bytes.

The contents and historical CI results above are bounded findings. Complete attribution of unmarked snippets, transitive dependencies, definitive patent status, rights-holder authority, full corresponding source and legal interpretation are not proven. See the linked detailed research documents and TASK-32/34/36/38/40/42/54/55/56 for evidence and unresolved conditions.
